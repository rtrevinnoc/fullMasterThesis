2. Mathematical Reconciliation (The "Teacher's Paradox")
Paper Equation (2.10): Mjp̈j + Njṗj + Qjpj = uj

If pj contains a 100mm displacement and Qj is 1012 N/m, the term Qjpj is indeed ~1011 N.
However, the Control Wrench uj is defined as:
uj = Qjpref + Mjp̈ref + ...

Substituting uj into (2.10) yields the Error Dynamics:
Mjëj + Njėj + Qj(pj - pref) = 0

The motor only produces force against the Tracking Error (e.g., 1nm), not the total distance.
